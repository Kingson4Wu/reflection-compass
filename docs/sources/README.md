# Article sources and provenance

Reflection Compass was informed by Dan Koe's _How to Fix Your Entire Life in 1 Day_. The product uses direction, a small action, and feedback as a lightweight loop; it does not implement the entire article as a daily questionnaire.

## Attribution and product boundaries

Dan Koe and other quoted authors retain attribution to their material. The source archive is excluded from the project's MIT license; see [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Reflection Compass is independent and is not endorsed by the article's author.

The article is an influence on design, not a guarantee of behavior change. The app leaves reflection optional, makes constraints explicit, and allows returning after a break without penalties. It does not assign psychological stages, infer why someone has not acted, or promise a life transformation in a day.
